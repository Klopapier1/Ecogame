    Rules
    • You have 50 rounds and start with 1000 gold. Whoever has the most gold at the end wins. Goods you haven't sold count for nothing.
    • A mine produces 3 uncut gems (20%) or 2 silver (80%) each round.
    • A jeweller cuts 2 uncut gems per round. Each cut fails 10% of the time and the gem is lost. Otherwise you get a sapphire (80%) or a diamond (20%).
    • Each mine or jeweller you already own makes the next one 15% more expensive.
    • An upgrade costs 300 gold, up to 3 per building. Each one adds +20% output and +10 points to the rare outcome (gems / diamonds). On a jeweller it also cuts the failure rate by 3 points.
    • Prices: diamond 150, sapphire 80, silver 40, uncut gem 30.
    • Buildings produce when you press End round, including ones you bought that round. Mines produce first and jewellers then cut from your stock, best-upgraded jewellers first.
