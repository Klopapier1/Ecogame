      Rules
      • You have 25 rounds and start with 1000 gold, plus one perk you pick from three. Whoever has the most gold at the end wins. Goods you haven't sold count for nothing.
      • Mine sites: each round 3 sites are offered. Hillside mines are average, silver veins produce almost only silver, gem pockets give lots of gems but run dry, rich lodes are strong but also run dry, and deep shafts are expensive but produce a lot. Each mine you already own makes the next one 12% more expensive.
      • Jeweller: cuts 2 uncut gems a round. Each cut fails 10% of the time and the gem is lost. Otherwise you get a sapphire (80%) or a diamond (20%).
      • Goldsmith: crafts 2 pieces a round, either a ring (1 silver + 1 sapphire) or a necklace (2 silver + 1 diamond). On auto, it makes whichever is worth more than its materials at current prices, and nothing if neither is.
      • An upgrade costs 300, up to 3 per building, and adds +20% output. On a mine it also adds +10 points to the gem chance. On a jeweller it adds +10 points to the diamond chance and lowers the failure rate by 3 points.
      • Market: each unit you sell lowers that good's price, and the price recovers 35% of the way back each round. Prices also drift at random, with occasional rushes and crashes.
      • Auto-sell: set a minimum price for a good. After the market moves each round, and as soon as you set the order, units are sold one at a time while the price is at or above your limit.
      • Events: some rounds bring an event that you must decide on before ending the round.
      • Upkeep: each mine costs 15–30 a round depending on its site type, a jeweller 20 and a goldsmith 30. Each building after the first adds 5% overhead on top. If you can't pay, your most valuable goods are sold automatically. You can sell a building for 50% of what you paid for it.
      • Warehouse: holds 40 goods to start. Anything over the limit at the end of a round is lost, cheapest goods first.
      • Order at the end of each round: mines, then jewellers, then goldsmiths (best-upgraded first), then the warehouse limit, then upkeep and debts, then the market moves, then auto-sell orders run.
