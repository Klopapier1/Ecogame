# Gem Tycoon

A single-file browser economy game. Open `index.html` in any browser, including on a phone. It has no build step and no dependencies.

You get 25 rounds and 500 starting gold, enough for exactly one building. Mines produce uncut gems or silver, and you can also buy both on the market at a markup. Jewellers cut gems into sapphires or diamonds, goldsmiths craft rings and necklaces, mints strike silver into coins, and glassworks melt gems into stained glass. Each of those four needs a guild licence, and you can only join 2 guilds per game, so you have to specialise. Sticking to a single guild boosts its output by 25%. Buildings produce nothing without workers. You work in one yourself for free, and you hire the rest from a few new applicants each round. Their skill varies, their wage is roughly proportional to skill give or take 30%, and they learn on the job: new to a task they manage 85% of their skill, levelling off around 120%. A second worker in the same building only adds half their skill. Every sale pushes prices down, though each market's buyers take a few units off it every round, every building costs upkeep, every hired worker draws a wage, and each different kind of building (every mine site type counts as its own kind) adds overhead. Each game starts with a choice of perks, mines come as varied sites (some run dry), random events force decisions, and standing sell orders handle routine selling. Horse carts with a driver take goods to the capital, a bigger and more volatile market that pays more for luxuries, and come back with the gold 2 rounds later. The goal is to finish with the most gold. The full rules are in the in-game "Rules" panel. All the tunable numbers are in the `CFG` object at the top of the script.

The leaderboard (top 10) and the game in progress are saved in the browser's localStorage.
